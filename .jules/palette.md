## 2026-04-02 - [A11y & Keyboard UX]
**Learning:** Standard keyboard shortcuts (`Ctrl/Cmd + Enter`) and clear ARIA labeling significantly improve the perceived professional quality and accessibility of a productivity workspace without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always include keyboard shortcut hints in tooltips for primary actions and ensure `role="toolbar"` and `role="textbox"` are used for custom editor components.
