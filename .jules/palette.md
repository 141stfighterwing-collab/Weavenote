## 2026-04-15 - [Shortcut Discovery & A11y Polish]
**Learning:** Users often prefer keyboard-centric workflows for rapid capture. Adding `Cmd/Ctrl+Enter` to all input entry points (title, tags, editor) significantly reduces friction. Micro-UX improvements like shortcut hints in tooltips bridge the gap between hidden power features and discoverability.
**Action:** Always include keyboard shortcut hints in primary action button tooltips and ensure icon-only buttons have explicit ARIA labels for screen reader users.
