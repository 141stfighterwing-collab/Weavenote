## 2026-04-23 - [Keyboard Shortcuts & Accessibility Enhancements]
**Learning:** Users in productivity-focused applications like note-taking apps highly benefit from standard keyboard shortcuts (like `Cmd/Ctrl + Enter` for saving) and clear visual/auditory cues for icon-only buttons.
**Action:** Always include keyboard shortcut hints in tooltips for primary actions and ensure all icon-only buttons have descriptive `aria-label` and `title` attributes. Use `role="toolbar"` for groups of formatting tools to provide better context for assistive technologies.
