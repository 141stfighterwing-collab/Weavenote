## 2024-04-22 - [Keyboard Shortcuts & ARIA Enhancement]
**Learning:** Adding `(Ctrl+Enter)` keyboard shortcut hints to the `title` tooltips of primary action buttons significantly improves discoverability of efficiency features without cluttering the UI. Icon-only buttons always require both `title` (for mouse users) and `aria-label` (for screen readers) to be fully accessible.
**Action:** Always include keyboard shortcut hints in tooltips for primary actions and ensure all icon-only buttons have descriptive ARIA labels.
